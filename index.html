💡 How Character Advancement Works in BESM 4E:

  • Stat Upgrade: 2 XP per +1 point (up to 12); 4 XP per +1 point (13+).
  • Skill Groups: Background 1 XP/lvl, Field 2 XP/lvl, Action 3 XP/lvl.
  • Attributes: Cost equals the attribute's CP rate per Level (1 to 10 XP/lvl).
  • Buying Off Defects: Spending XP equal to the defect's refund value removes the defect.

Awarded XP directly expands your Character Point allowance in the Character Builder tab so you can upgrade stats, attributes, and skill groups immediately.

🎲 BESM 4E Action Resolution

Standard Stat / Action Check (Roll-Over):

Result = 2d6 + Stat / CV + Modifiers ≥ Target Number (TN)

Roll two six-sided dice, add your relevant Stat or Combat Value, and compare to the Target Number (TN). If your total meets or exceeds the TN, the action succeeds!

🌟 Edges and Obstacles

Tactical advantages or environmental handicaps modify the dice pool:

  • Minor Edge: Roll 3d6, keep the 2 highest dice.
  • Major Edge: Roll 4d6, keep the 2 highest dice.
  • Minor Obstacle: Roll 3d6, keep the 2 lowest dice.
  • Major Obstacle: Roll 4d6, keep the 2 lowest dice.

⚡ Dramatic Feats (EP Burn)

A character can dig deep and spend 10 Energy Points (EP) to gain a +1 bonus to any die roll, up to a maximum bonus equal to their Soul Stat (e.g. Soul 5 = up to +5 bonus for 50 EP).

⚔️ Opposed Combat Resolution

  • Attacker: Rolls 2d6 + Attack Combat Value (ACV).
  • Defender: Rolls 2d6 + Defence Combat Value (DCV).
  • Hit Resolution: Attacker hits if their total is greater than or equal to (≥) the Defender's total (ties go to the attacker).
  • Critical Hits: Rolling a natural 12 on the kept pair is a Critical Triumph. Rolling a natural 2 is a Critical Fumble.

🛡️ Damage & Armour Absorption

Total Damage dealt = Weapon Level × Damage Multiplier (DM). Armour Rating (AR) subtracts directly from incoming damage. Remaining damage is deducted from Health Points (HP).
